# Posts index

Scope: the writing index at /posts (and its paged routes). Visitor mode: Read inside an Experience habit; a reviewer skims titles and dates, picks one, and opens it behind the curtain.

Audience and job: a hiring team scanning what Miles writes about, one click from a post.

Source of approval: on 2026-10-14 the user asked to retire the post cards (every post's hero is textural and abstract, so a framed picture per card adds nothing) and chose "Projection room" over Strata and Credits roll through the structured question. Later that day the user found the left-edge alignment odd and the titles compressed: the list moved to a centred column with a wider title measure.

## Direction contract

THESIS: /posts is a projection room. The titles are the content; the post's texture is the light the room is lit by, never a picture in a frame. Refuses cards, thumbnails, a picture per row, cursor-following image lenses.

OWN-WORLD: the site's ink-on-paper system and the light (300) titles of /works. One sticky full-bleed ground under the list carries the shown post's hero as the opening resolves it, on the visitor's theme (pins dropped), graded by a scrim of the page's own background: dense under a centred column (66rem), open toward both browser edges. Titles light at up to 3rem on a 30ch measure. Uploads whose value fights the theme print as a grey negative. Mono meta column: month, rule, source.

STORY: the reader reads "Posts" and its count, the first post's texture already lit; scrolling moves each title through the reading line, and the ground dissolves to its texture with a slow push-in; on a fine pointer, hovering takes the light; resting wakes the live field under the type.

FIRST VIEWPORT: heading and the first titles in a centred column, the shown one in full ink and the rest dimmed, the first texture open on both flanks.

FORM: user-chosen "Projection room"; no concept roll (structured-question answer, 2026-10-14).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review and its verdict.
