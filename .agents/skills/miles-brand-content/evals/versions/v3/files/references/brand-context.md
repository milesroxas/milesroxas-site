# Personal Brand: Miles Roxas

## Canonical identity
- **Title:** Designer & Builder
- **Disciplines:** Brand · Product · Technology
- **Core line:** I design experiences and build the systems behind them.
- **Positioning:** A hands-on, multidisciplinary designer who makes sense of complex problems, designs thoughtful solutions, and builds products, tools, systems, and digital experiences.
- **Differentiator:** Connects visual craft, creative direction, systems thinking, product judgment, and execution. Works from defining the problem through building, testing, and iteration.
- **Motivators:** Solving hard design and systems challenges; improving workflows and productivity; creating useful products; telling meaningful brand stories.
- **Belief:** Excellent design must combine craft, clarity, usability, and real-world impact.

## Background and career direction
- Brooklyn-based; **16 years** across brand, digital, product, design leadership, and development.
- Co-founder of **Suits & Sandals**, working hands-on with startups and enterprises while leading creative teams and delivery.
- Seeking a **full-time, hands-on role at a product organization** with deeper focus and ownership over time. Transitioning away from agency operations into a silent-partner role.
- Best-fit work is design-led but close to implementation: product/web design, design systems, interaction, creative technology, design engineering, or design-focused technical roles. Match the title to the actual job, not an assumed specialty.
- Leadership establishes experience and judgment; it should **not eclipse the identity as a maker**.

## AI and engineering positioning
- **AI is a tool in the workflow, not the professional identity.** It expands what can be explored and built, including complex applications and custom systems.
- Credit Miles for the work he actually owns: framing the problem, design, creative and technical direction, implementation where applicable, review, testing, and refinement.
- Be transparent about AI assistance, collaborators, and engineering limitations. Never imply conventional software-engineering expertise or authorship that cannot be substantiated.
- Emphasize decisions, tradeoffs, quality, and results rather than model names, prompts, or the volume of code produced.

## Evidence and subject areas
- **Suits & Sandals website / Immersive Lab:** Digital craft plus CMS, creative tools, interactive experiences, and AI-assisted content/documentation workflows.
- **Buzlee:** Bringing a community product from concept to working applications and supporting systems.
- **NextStreet / Carbon Signal:** Simplifying complex organizational or product needs through design and systems thinking.
- **Brand experiences (e.g., Arturo, Vault):** Identity, storytelling, expressive design, and interactive narratives.
- **Independent experiments (e.g., Darkfiber):** Curiosity about novel interfaces, interactive systems, and emerging technology. Clearly label experiments versus shipped work.
- **Verify every public claim** about scope, personal contribution, dates, metrics, and outcomes against the latest case study or primary evidence.

## Voice and style
- **Sound like:** A thoughtful, curious, experienced designer sharing work and lessons. Confident, direct, personal, calm, grounded, and conversational.
- **Write:** Short, concrete sentences; plain English; strong verbs; useful specifics. Show thinking and craft without overexplaining.
- **Avoid:** Buzzwords, agency sales language, corporate jargon, self-congratulation, grand claims, AI hype, clickbait, forced inspiration, and generic thought leadership.
- **Never use em dashes.** Avoid inflated adjectives, filler, and cliché openings.
- **Point of view:** Use **I** for individual work and **we** for genuine collaboration; credit others accurately.
- **Preferred narrative:** Problem → insight → choices → what was built → outcome → lesson. Make the reader understand **why** something was made, not merely **what** was made.

## Content pillars
1. **Design and problem solving:** Research, UX, visual systems, interaction, design decisions, and tradeoffs.
2. **Tools and systems:** Applications, automation, internal tooling, workflows, and productivity.
3. **Brand and experiences:** Identity, storytelling, motion, immersive web, and visual craft.
4. **Building and learning:** Process, experiments, technical direction, AI-assisted development, testing, and lessons from shipping.

## Channel instructions
- **Portfolio / website:** Lead with **Designer & Builder**. Prove the positioning through selected work, decisions, artifacts, and outcomes. Avoid a generic list of agency services.
- **LinkedIn / social:** Brief, personal, specific. Share something built, a problem solved, a decision made, or a useful lesson. Give readers a reason to explore the full work. Don't make every post promotional.
- **YouTube:** Calm, friendly, visually demonstrative, builder-to-builder. Show the actual work and practical decisions. Prioritize clarity and retention without hype or dunking on other tools.
- **CV / applications / interviews:** Emphasize hands-on design, systems thinking, execution, collaboration, and business impact. Explain the deliberate move from running an agency to contributing deeply to one product. Tailor evidence to each role and never exaggerate engineering depth.

## Instructions for all agents
1. Preserve **Designer & Builder** as the central identity; adapt emphasis, not underlying facts, to each audience.
2. Make **design the foundation** and **building how ideas become real**. Don't default to “software engineer,” “creative director,” or “AI expert” as the primary label.
3. Lead with the problem, audience, reasoning, and value before naming tools or frameworks.
4. Prefer concise, human language and concrete evidence over polished marketing claims.
5. **Never invent** roles, credentials, collaborators, clients, results, metrics, project status, or technical contributions.
6. Distinguish aspirational career positioning from verified experience; flag information that needs checking.
7. If new information conflicts with this document, verify and update the source of truth.
