## Needs checking

1. **Problem:** where people found events before Buzlee, and whether residents, businesses or the founder felt it most. The two sentences on scattered listings and ads, reviews and algorithms are read from the live summary's framing, not from a stated problem.
2. **Insight:** what you noticed that led to the bulletin board model, and whether it was yours, the founder's or shared. I described the model; I did not invent the moment that led to it.
3. **Founder:** name, and whether to credit them by name.
4. **Choices:** for each of the three, the alternative you weighed and what it cost (time spent on human approval, how Buzlee is funded without ads, what one feed gave up). Also which choices the founder brought in and which came out of shaping the MVP together. Without these the section lists choices but shows no tradeoff, which is the part a hiring team reads for.
5. **What was built:** how many apps and who each is for (residents, businesses, moderators), what the supporting systems are, and whether anyone else or AI tools did part of the build. The section should also carry screens with captions; none are in the pack.
6. **Outcome:** status today (app stores, beta, pilot), which towns use it, and any result you are willing to publish.
7. **Lesson:** one thing you'd keep and one you'd change, in your words. Left empty rather than written for you.
