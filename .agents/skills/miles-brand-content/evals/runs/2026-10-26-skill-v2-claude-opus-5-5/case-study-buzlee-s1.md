# Buzlee: local events without the noise

## Finding what's on in a small town

People in a small town want to know what is on this week, and the local businesses running the events want them to know. The listings rarely sit in one place. Where they do, ads, reviews and algorithms decide what deserves attention. [check: where people found events before Buzlee, and who felt the problem most: residents, businesses or the founder]

Buzlee is built to answer that one question.

## The town bulletin board

Buzlee works like a town bulletin board, made digital. A local business pins up a flyer, a person checks that it belongs, and everyone reads the same board. Nothing on it is ranked, rated or paid for. [check: what you noticed that led to this model, and whether it came from you, the founder or both]

## Shaping the MVP

I shaped the MVP with Buzlee's founder [check: the founder's name, and whether to name them]. It rests on three choices:

- **A person approves every post.** Local businesses post their events as flyers, and a person approves each one.
- **One feed and one map.** Everything on this week sits in a single feed and on a single map, so there is one place to look.
- **No ads, ratings or ranking.** Nothing is promoted, scored or reordered by an algorithm. The board shows what was posted and approved.
